Evidence model

Every conclusion states how strongly it is supported

Apple Confirmed

Supported by Apple documentation, release notes, WWDC, DTS, or an explicit Apple engineer statement.

Reproduced

Consistently observed in controlled testing, but not documented as a platform contract.

Inferred

Derived from logs, experiments, implementation behavior, or other engineering observations.
